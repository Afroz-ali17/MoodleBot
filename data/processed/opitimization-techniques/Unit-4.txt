Game:
In a broad sense, Game is not as a kind of sport, a competitive situation will be
called as Game.
OR
Game is an activity between two or more persons involving activities by each
person according to a predetermined set of rules, where at the end of the game,
each person receives some benefits or satisfaction or suffers loss (negative benefit).
Properties of game:
1. There is finite number of competitors called “players”.
2. Each of the participants has a series of plans or models of behavior or a finite
number of possible courses of action. i.e. has several choices as to his
appropriate actions however, there may not same for each competitor.
3. A play of the game takes place where each player employs it strategy.
4. The choices of all participants are assumed to be made simultaneously, so that
no competitor knows his opponent’s choice until he selects his own course of
action.
5. The outcome of the all specific sets of choice by all the competitors is known
in advance and defined numerically. The outcome of all combinations results
to each player (positive, negative or zero). The negative gain is considered as
loss.
6. The gain of a participant depends not only on his own actions, but also those of
others.
7. The player can make individual decisions without direct communications.
8. The game is said to be “fair game” if the value of game is zero, otherwise it is
known as “unfair”.
Game Theory
Game theory is a type of decision theory in which one’s choice of action is
determined after taking into account all possible alternatives available to an
opponent’s playing the same game rather than just by the possibilities of several
outcome results.
Game theory deals with competitive situations of decision making under
uncertainty. I t was first developed by John Von Neumann.( He is called as father
of game theory).

Application of game theory:
Game theory has been used to study a wide variety of human and
animal behaviors. It was initially developed economic behaviors, including
behaviors of firms, markets, and consumers. The use of game theory in the
social sciences has expanded, and game theory has been applied to political,
sociology, and psychological behaviors as well.
1) Political science: The application of game theory to political science is
focused in the overlapping areas of fair division, political economy,
public choice, positive political theory, and social choice theory. In each
of these areas, researchers have developed game theoretic model in
which the players are often voters, stares special interest groups, and
politicians.
2) Economics and business: Economists have long used game theory to
analyze a wide array of economic phenomena, including auctions,
bargaining, duopolies, fair division, oligopolies, social network
formation and voting systems.
3) Biology: unlike economics, the payoffs for games in biology are often
interpreted as corresponding to fitness. In addition, the focus has been
less on equilibrium that corresponds to a notion of rationality, but rather
on ones that would be maintained by evolutionary forces. The best
known equilibrium in biology is known as the evolutionarily stable
strategy.
4) Computer science and logic: Game theory has come to play an
increasingly important role in logic and in computer science. Several
logical theories have a basis in game semantics. In addition, computer
scientists have used games to model interactive computations. Also,
game theory provides a theoretical basis to the field of multi-agent
systems.

Sometimes, it is observed that one of pure strategies of either player is
inferior to at least one of the remaining ones. The superior strategies are said
to dominate the inferior ones. In such cases of dominance, we can reduce
the size of the payoff matrix by deleting those strategies which are
dominated by others.
The method of reducing mxn game to 2xn or mx2 or 2x2 games is
dominance property.
1. If each element in one row say ith row of the payoff matrix is less than or
equal to the corresponding element in the other row say Kth row, then the
player A will never choose the ith strategy. We can delete the ith row from
the payoff matrix.
2. If each element of one column says ith column is greater than or equal to
the corresponding column say kth column, then the player B will never
choose rth column. We can delete the rth column from the payoff matrix.
3. Dominance need not be based on superiority of pure strategies only a
given strategy can be dominated, if it is inferior to an average of two or
more other pure strategies.
In general if some convex combination of some rows dominance the
ith row then irh row will be deleted.
If the ith row dominance the convex combination of some other rows
then one of the rows involving in the combinations may be deleted.
Similar rules follow the columns also.
Method of game without saddle point with mixed strategies:
In certain cases, no pure strategy solutions exist for the game. In other
words, saddle points do not exist. In such a game , both players may
adopt an optimal blend of the strategies called “Mixed strategy” to find a
saddle point because they are the probabilistic combination of the choice
strategy. The optima mix for each player may be determined by
assigning each strategy a probability of it being chose. Thus, these mixed
strategies are probabilistic combinations of available better (or dominant)
strategies and these games hence called “probabilistic games”.
Similarly, when saddle points do not exist in the game, we can use
the dominance principle to reduce to 2x2 game or at least 2xn or mx2
matrices. If this is also not possible we can formulate it as linear
programming problem.

Limitations of game theory:
1. Unrealistic assumption: the assumption that the players know their own
as well as other’s (opponent’s) payoff is unrealistic. One can only guess
of his own.
2. Complexity with more number of players: as the number of players
increase, the problem becomes so complex that it is becomes difficult to
solve.
3. Minimax- maximin is impractical: the assumption of maximin-minimax
show that the players are risk-averse and have thorough knowledge of
strategies, which seems impractical.
4. Limitations on collusion: the game theory cannot be applicable in case of
players share their business secrets to work out collusion.
5. Non-dynamic or static nature: In markets, generally the changes are very
frequent and fast. The game theory least scope to work out the strategy
Immediately unless some ground work to known payoff is made.
6. No base for calculating payoff: There is any correct formula and
scientific base for calculating payoffs game.
