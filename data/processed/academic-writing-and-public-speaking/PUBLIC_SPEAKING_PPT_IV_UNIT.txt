Public speaking is the process of speaking to a group of people in a structured, deliberate manner intended to inform, influence, or entertain the listeners. It is closely allied to "presenting", although the latter has more of a commercial connotation.
Characteristics of Public Speaking
Effective public speaking is characterized by clear, structured, and engaging communication designed to inform, persuade, or entertain an audience. Key traits include confidence, audience awareness, authentic delivery, and thorough preparation. It requires adapting to feedback, using effective, concise language, and maintaining engagement through voice and body language.
How to Communicate?
Imagine you are about to communicate through WhatsApp or any other platform. First, you think about the person you are going to speak to.
Based on who that person is, your tone, choice of words, and even the medium you select naturally change.
After choosing the appropriate channel, the next step is to craft your message.
Organize ideas, frame them clearly, and place the message into the selected channel.
The channel then carries and delivers the message to the receiver.
Once the ideation stage is complete, communication progresses from forming the idea to creating the message, selecting the channel, and finally transmitting it.

4PS of Communication
Plan
A good presentation begins with a strong foundation, that’s what we emphatize in our article on how to structure a presentation . So to accomplish this good foundation, we start with the planning. Before you open PowerPoint or design a single slide, ask yourself:
Who is my audience?
What do they expect or need to learn?
What is my main message?
What action do I want them to take
Prepare
Once the outline is ready, it’s time to prepare slides and supporting content. Think of the slides as visual aids that enhance—not replace—your spoken message.
Focus on visual story telling not just bullet points. Use presentation diagrams, images, charts, and infographics to illustrate key points. This makes your presentation more engaging and memorable.
Stick to a consistent color scheme and font style.
Limit text per slide (aim for clarity over quantity).
Practice
Rehearse to Refine
Even the most beautifully designed slides won’t save you if your delivery is off. This is where practice becomes essential.
Rehearsing helps you:
Refine your timing.
Improve transitions between sections.
Catch awkward phrasing.
Feel more confident and less nervous.
Perform
Deliver with Impact
Presentation day is here. Now it’s all about the performance. This goes beyond what you say—it includes how you say it.
Performance Tips:
Maintain eye contact and smile to build connection.
Use natural gestures and confident posture.
Vary your vocal tone and pace to emphasize key points.
Pause to let important ideas sink in.

Answering Strategies
Be Direct: Answer the specific question asked, not a different one.
Keep it Simple: Use plain language and concise points.
Reinforce Key Message: Weave your main argument into the answer when possible.
Use Evidence: Support points with data or brief stories.
Don't Bluff: If you don't know, say "That's a great question, and I'll need to follow up with you on that".
